Check that the cluster formed before anything else:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app={{ include "promovolve.fullname" . }}-api
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec sts/{{ include "promovolve.fullname" . }}-api -- \
    wget -qO- localhost:8558/cluster/members

A pod that stays Running and NotReady has not joined. That is the designed
behaviour, not a hang -- readiness means cluster membership, and the startup
probe deliberately watches /alive so a slow join is not mistaken for a dead
process and restarted into a loop.
